## Overview
A small-window launcher accessible from your Quick Settings or home screen!  
Arrange your apps and shortcuts exactly how you like.  

This "small-window launcher" can be opened instantly from your Quick Settings panel or home screen. Group your frequently used apps and shortcuts into a small window, allowing you to launch them quickly—right on top of whatever screen you are currently using.  

It can also function as a folder feature for Soulful Launcher!  
Exclusively for Soulful Launcher, you can even add shortcuts directly from your home screen.  

## App Features


### ■ Designed for ease of use as a standalone launcher!
・Place up to 12 apps or shortcuts (depending on the app) in a single small window.  
・Customize the layout, addition direction, and the number of rows and columns.  
・Add half-cell spacing for a clean, organized look.  
・Adjust icon size, window size, and the colors and transparency of the borders, background, and cells.  
・Toggle app name visibility on or off.  
・Includes a feature to add a tile to your Quick Settings panel (tap it to open the small window instantly).  
・Choose from various icons, images, or colors when adding it to your home screen.  

### ■ Recommended for

Those looking for a simple, easy-to-use launcher.  
Those who want to place a launcher in a convenient spot on their screen.  
Those who want a "folder feature" for Soulful Launcher.
