The app connects to the internet for the following purposes.  
・To access the pages of the links you saved and obtain their titles and thumbnail images (when saving, when re-fetching information, and for automatic retrieval after an import)

As with any normal access, the operators of those sites can see information such as your device’s IP address. For how those sites handle information, please refer to their own policies.  
Of the content of the pages obtained, the app uses only the title and images, and does not run scripts or other code in the pages. The link check excludes sites that cannot be checked correctly, such as X and Instagram.

・When you use “Share” or “Send”, the app hands the link URL or folder contents to the app you choose. How the receiving app handles the information is governed by that app.  
・Text or files shared from other apps are saved in the app only when you confirm the import.  
・When you “send a folder as a file”, the app creates a file for sending in a temporary area on your device. Files older than one day are deleted automatically.  
・When you open a link, the app launches the browser or app that you chose, or that is set on your device.

The app obtains from Google Play whether a purchase has been made, and uses it on your device to determine whether premium features are available. If a purchase is refunded, premium features become unavailable the next time the app checks the purchase status. Purchase information can be restored between devices using the same Google account, based on your Google Play purchase history.

The app’s settings and data are, in principle, stored on your device and are not sent to or stored on our servers. If you uninstall the app from your device, the data on the device is deleted.
